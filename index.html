Privacy Policy

We built this app with privacy first. Your trade data belongs to you — it lives on your device and goes nowhere without your knowledge.

Developer: Alfonso Bergamini Last updated: April 2026 App: Track Your Profit

Data we collect

Trade data
Dates, asset names, entry/exit prices, and P&L values that you enter. All stored exclusively on your device — never uploaded to our servers.
Local device only
Your name
The first name you enter during onboarding, used only to personalise greetings inside the app. Never transmitted anywhere.
Local device only
Trading preferences
Your trading style, biggest challenge, and goals — collected during onboarding to personalise your dashboard. Stored locally only.
Local device only
Subscription status
Whether you have an active Pro subscription. Managed by RevenueCat, which uses an anonymous app user ID — not linked to your name or email.

What we never collect

Email address or any contact information
Location, camera, microphone, or contacts
Device identifiers or advertising IDs
Browsing history or analytics
Personal information sent to the AI Coach

Third-party services

RevenueCat
Processes in-app subscription payments using an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or trade data.
RevenueCat Privacy Policy →
CLAUDE AI (AI Coach feature)
When you tap "Analyze my trades", your trade statistics (dates, symbols, P&L values) are sent to Claude API to generate insights. No personal information is included — only the anonymized trading numbers you have already entered. You can review CLaude data handling below.
Google Play Billing
Payments made through the Google Play Store are processed by Google. We never see or store your payment card details. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Data storage & security

Stays on your device
All trade history, your name, and preferences are stored in your device's local storage using standard app storage. There is no Track Your Profit server or database that holds your data.
We never sell your data
Your trade data and personal information are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers — ever.
No personal data shared with third parties
The only external data transfer is anonymized trade statistics to NVIDIA when you explicitly use the AI Coach feature. No name, no email, no device ID is ever included.

Your rights

1
Delete all data — go to Settings → Clear all trades to permanently erase all trade history from your device.
2
Opt out of AI Coach — simply don't tap "Analyze my trades". No data is ever sent to NVIDIA unless you explicitly trigger that feature.
3
Uninstall — removing the app from your device deletes all locally stored data permanently.
4
Contact us with any privacy question at takeyourprofit.info@gmail.com and we will respond within 5 business days.
